Usage

Keep clear space of at least two book-stroke widths around the visible logo. Use navy on light surfaces and white on dark surfaces. Gold is a secondary display option. Never stretch, rotate, add shadows, or change the symbol geometry. Use the symbol below 120px wide; use descriptor versions at 280px or wider.

See README.md for formats, color values and printing notes.
